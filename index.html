Know what’s done before everyone clocks out.

CloseOut is a mobile-first closing task tracker for small independent restaurants. Staff tick off end-of-night jobs while supervisors get one clear view of what is finished and what still needs attention.

Try the demo
The problem

Closing should not depend on memory.

For closing supervisors and staff in small, single-location restaurants, end-of-night work can be split across floor, bar, kitchen and supervisor duties. When the process relies on verbal reminders, memory or paper lists, it is harder to see the whole close at a glance.

Who uses it?

Closing supervisors and closing staff working across floor, bar and kitchen.

Who decides?

The restaurant owner or general manager chooses whether to adopt the system.

What they need

A fast, phone-friendly view of tasks, progress and unfinished work before the team leaves.

How it works

Three steps. No training manual required.

1. Open

The team opens the closing checklist on a phone and sees tonight’s tasks.

2. Complete

Staff tick jobs as they finish them. CloseOut automatically recalculates progress.

3. Check

The supervisor filters by area and sees exactly what remains before lock-up.
